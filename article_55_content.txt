<!-- wp:heading {"level":1} -->
<h1 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What is the Reporting Hub in CMGalaxy?</strong></h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <strong>Reporting Hub</strong> is CMGalaxy’s centralized reporting engine designed to generate, automate, manage, and share performance reports across all your connected marketing platforms — powered by AI insights from <strong>Lex</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It eliminates manual data compilation and transforms raw campaign data into structured, share-ready reports in minutes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Overview</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Modern marketing teams operate across multiple platforms — Google, Meta, LinkedIn, Amazon, X, DV360, Pinterest, CDP, and more. Each platform provides isolated dashboards, making cross-platform visibility complex and time-consuming.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <strong>Reporting Hub</strong> solves this by:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Consolidating performance reporting in one place</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Generating AI-powered insights automatically</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Enabling scheduled automated reporting</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Providing multi-format export options (PDF, CSV)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Allowing secure report sharing (Email, WhatsApp, In-App)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Delivering real-time report status notifications</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Users can access recommended AI reports and manage all reports from a centralized dashboard.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
